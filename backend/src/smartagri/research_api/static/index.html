Advice

V0–V5 is the ablation ladder: each rung adds one layer, and V5 is the full model.

Answers are in English by default. For answers in the local language (Latin script), start the server with SMARTAGRI_LOCALE=sr-Latn or choose it in Settings. Questions in English or the local language are recognised by keyword rules; when a question is not recognised, the system asks which type of advice you need, or you can pick it above. Sources stay in their original language: the passages of the published corpus are in Serbian.

Free AI conversation baseline VC
